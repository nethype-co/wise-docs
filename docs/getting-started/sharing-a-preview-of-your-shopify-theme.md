## Sharing a preview of your Shopify theme

Before you publish Wise, you can share a preview link with your team, a client or our support team. Anyone with the link can see the unpublished theme in their browser, without access to your Shopify admin.

## Share a preview link

1. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Find Wise in your **Theme library**.
3. Click the **...** button next to the theme and choose **Preview**.
4. In the preview bar at the bottom of the page, click **Share preview**.
5. Click **Copy link** and send the link to anyone you like.

You can also share a preview from the theme editor: click the **...** button in the top bar and choose **Share preview**.

:::note
* Preview links expire after a while. If a link no longer works, create a new one.
* If your store is password protected, visitors need the store password to see the preview.
* The preview shows the last saved version of the theme. Click **Save** in the theme editor before you share the link.
:::

For more detail, see [Shopify Help Center: Previewing themes](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/managing-themes/previewing-themes).

## Free trial

You can try Wise for free in your Shopify admin before you buy it. The trial gives you full access to every feature, section and setting, so you can test the theme with your own products and content.

## Purchases and refunds

All theme purchases go through the **Shopify Theme Store** and follow Shopify's purchase terms. Because themes are digital products, Shopify applies a **no-refund policy** to all paid theme purchases. This policy is shown at checkout.

For any billing questions, including purchases, charges or refunds, contact [Shopify Support](https://help.shopify.com/) directly. All payments and billing are handled by Shopify.

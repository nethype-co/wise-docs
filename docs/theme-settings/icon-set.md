## Icon set

<SectionMeta version="v1.0.0" />

The **Icon set** settings control the icons used across your store: the header icons, the icons on buttons, the open and close sign of accordions, the quick buy icon on product cards and the round close cursor. You'll find them under **Theme settings** > **Icon set** in the theme editor. To open theme settings, click the gear (settings) icon in the left sidebar of the theme editor.

## Settings

#### Choose icons for navigation

* **Icon set:** The style of all the theme's icons (options: Wise icon set, Monsoon icon set). Switching the set changes every icon in the store at once, such as the menu, search, account, cart, arrows and close icons.
* **Enable bag icon in buy button:** Show a bag icon on the add to cart button on the product page, in the sticky buy button and on compact product card buttons.
* **Enable lock icon in checkout button:** Show a lock icon on the checkout button in the cart, as a sign of a secure checkout.

#### Set custom icons

Upload your own images to replace the header icons. Leave a field empty to use the icon from the selected icon set.

* **Menu icon:** The icon that opens the menu drawer.
* **Search icon:** The icon that opens the search.
* **Account icon:** The icon that links to the customer account.
* **Bag icon:** The cart icon.

:::tip
Header icons are shown at about 22 px. Upload small square images, ideally SVG or PNG with a transparent background, at least 60 x 60 px so they stay sharp on high resolution screens. Uploaded images keep their own colors and do not follow the header text color.
:::

#### Accordions

* **Accordion indicator:** The sign used to open and close every collapsible row in the theme, for example in FAQs, product descriptions and footer menus on mobile (options: Arrow, Plus / minus).

#### Pro card

* **Quick buy icon:** The icon on the quick buy button on product cards (options: Plus, Cart).
* **Compact card quick buy icon:** The icon on the buy button of compact product cards set to the icon style (options: Plus, Cart).

#### Close cursor

* **Display custom close cursor:** On computers with a mouse, show a round close icon that follows the pointer when it moves over the dimmed area around an open drawer, popup or search. It shows customers that clicking there closes the panel.
* **Cursor background** and **Cursor icon color:** The colors of the round close cursor. Shown only when the setting above is on.

## Localization drawer

<SectionMeta version="v1.0.0" />

The localization drawer is the panel where customers choose their country (and with it the currency) and their language. It opens from the country and language selectors in the header and footer. You'll find its settings under **Theme settings** > **Localization drawer** in the theme editor. To open theme settings, click the gear (settings) icon in the left sidebar of the theme editor.

## Settings

#### Localization drawer content

* **Show flag in localization drawer:** Show a small country flag next to each country in the drawer and next to the current country on the selector button in the header or footer. Turn it off for a text-only selector.

:::note
The selector only appears when your store sells in more than one country or language. Add countries in your Shopify admin under **Settings** > **Markets**, and languages under **Settings** > **Languages**. Then add the country or language selector to the [Header](/header-footer/header) or [Footer](/header-footer/footer).
:::

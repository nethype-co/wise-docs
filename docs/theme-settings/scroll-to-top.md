## Scroll to top

<SectionMeta version="v1.0.0" />

The scroll to top button is a round button in the bottom corner of the screen. It appears once a customer scrolls down the page and takes them back to the top with one click, which is handy on long collection and product pages. You'll find its settings under **Theme settings** > **Scroll to top** in the theme editor. To open theme settings, click the gear (settings) icon in the left sidebar of the theme editor.

## Settings

* **Enable scroll to top button:** Show the button. It stays hidden at the top of the page and fades in after the customer scrolls down a little.
* **Button color style:** Use the colors of your primary or secondary buttons (options: Primary, Secondary). You set those colors in [Colors](/theme-settings/colors) under **Buttons**.

#### Position

* **Horizontal spacing:** The distance between the button and the edge of the screen (0 to 100 px).
* **Align to left side:** Place the button in the bottom left corner instead of the bottom right. Useful when a chat widget or another app already uses the bottom right corner.

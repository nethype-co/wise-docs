## Favicon

<SectionMeta version="v1.0.0" />

The favicon is the small icon shown next to your store name in the browser tab, in bookmarks and in the browser history. You'll find it under **Theme settings** > **Favicon** in the theme editor. To open theme settings, click the gear (settings) icon in the left sidebar of the theme editor.

{/* TODO: screenshot of a browser tab with the favicon */}

## Settings

* **Favicon image:** Upload the image to use as your favicon. It is scaled down to 32 x 32 px.

:::tip
Use a simple, square image with a transparent background, for example your logo mark without the text. Fine details and small text are hard to see at 32 x 32 px.
:::
